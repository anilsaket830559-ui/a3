Planning Your Semester

Four or five syllabi make one semester. Planning turns them into a single, realistic week-by-week schedule you can actually follow.

  • 5 units
  • About 8 minutes
  • Reviewed November 2026

Unit 1

Gather every course outline

Collect all your syllabi in one place on the first day of term. Make a single list of every assessed task with its course, weight and due date, sorted by date. This list becomes the spine of your plan.

Unit 2

Find the pinch points

Look for weeks where several deadlines cluster.

  • Highlight any week with two or more major deadlines.
  • Start those tasks two to three weeks earlier.
  • Use the semester planner on our home page to see the shape of the term.

Unit 3

Build a weekly template

Block in classes, work and travel first, then add regular study blocks for each course, roughly two hours of study for every hour of class. Keep the same blocks each week so studying becomes a habit rather than a decision.

Unit 4

Review every Sunday

Spend fifteen minutes each week checking what is due in the next fortnight and adjusting your study blocks. A short, regular review stops small delays from becoming a crisis at the end of term.

Unit 5

Protect rest and buffer time

Leave at least one evening free each week and some slack before exams. Plans fail when every hour is booked; buffer time absorbs illness, extra reading and the unexpected.
